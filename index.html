Chart method
Takeoff uses the next higher published chart weight, pressure altitude and temperature. Landing uses the published 2,550 lb short-field chart; actual landing weight is shown separately.
Wind corrections
Headwind decreases distance 10% per 9 kt. Tailwind increases distance 10% per 2 kt, up to 10 kt. Dry grass adds 15% of takeoff ground roll or 45% of landing ground roll.
Runway inputs
TORA/TODA/LDA are displayed for the Seneca worksheet and runway checks. Slopes, wet/contaminated surfaces and unlisted conditions are not modeled.
